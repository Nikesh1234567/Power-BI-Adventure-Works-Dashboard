# Adventure Works Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
An end-to-end Power BI solution developed for Adventure Works, a global manufacturing company. This 6-page report provides deep insights into sales performance, product trends, and customer behavior, utilizing advanced data modeling and AI-driven visuals.

## 📄 Report Pages & Insights

### 1. Executive Summary (Main Page)
*   **High-Level KPIs:** Real-time tracking of Revenue, Profit, Orders, and Return Rate.
*   **Performance vs. Previous Month:** Dynamic KPI cards for Monthly Revenue, Orders, and Return rates.
*   **Product Insights:** Ranking of top products by Orders, Revenue, and Return Rate.
*   **Time Series:** Revenue trending over time via interactive line charts.

### 2. Geographic Distribution (Map Visual)
*   Global sales overview tracking **Total Orders by Country** to identify high-growth territories.

### 3. Product Performance & Goal Tracking
*   **Target Monitoring:** Gauge visuals comparing actual vs. target for Orders, Revenue, and Profit.
*   **What-If Analysis:** Line charts comparing **Profit vs. Adjusted Profit** using numeric range parameters to simulate price/cost changes.

### 4. Customer Intelligence
*   **Key Metrics:** Unique Customer count and Revenue per Customer.
*   **Behavioral Tracking:** Weekly customer acquisition trends.
*   **Deep Dive:** A comprehensive Matrix visual ranking customers by total orders and revenue, filtered by **Yearly Slicer**.

### 5. Root Cause Analysis (Decomposition Tree)
*   AI-powered breakdown of **Return Rates** across Categories, Sub-categories, and specific Product Names to identify quality issues.

### 6. Predictive Insights (Key Influencers)
*   Advanced AI analysis to determine the primary factors influencing business outcomes, such as the **likelihood of customer home ownership** and its impact on sales.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
*   **Modeling:** Star Schema with 1-to-many relationships.
*   **DAX:** Time intelligence functions, Target-setting measures, and Parameter-based logic.
*   **AI Visuals:** Decomposition Tree and Key Influencers.
*   **Features:** Bookmarks, Slicers, Tooltips, and What-If Parameters.
